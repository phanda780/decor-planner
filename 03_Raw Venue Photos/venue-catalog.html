Sagan & Shaadi

Main Lawn — Sagan & Shaadi

The primary event space: a massive open ground at the back of the property. Sagan evening (Nov 1, 7 PM) and Main Wedding (Nov 2, 4 PM sundowner).

🌍 Raw dirt/mud surface — no grass yet 📐 ~14,600 sq ft open area 🏔 Himalayan mountain backdrop on multiple sides 🏨 Hotel building flanks the left edge 🌄 Open sky — ideal for sundowner lighting 🚛 Currently under construction (trucks visible)
📐 Planning Note The lawn is photographed empty and raw — this is pre-construction. The surface will be finished before November. These shots capture the full scale and proportions you're working with. The hotel building on the left and the mountain ridgeline to the right/front are the permanent features to plan decor around.
